## Community Support (Free)

Community support is available through public channels. Response times are
best-effort and depend on maintainer availability.

| Channel | Use For | Expected Response |
|:---|:---|:---|
| [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/craton-co/craton-shield-enterprise/issues) | Bug reports, feature requests | 1-5 business days |
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/craton-co/craton-shield-enterprise/discussions) | Questions, design discussions | Best effort |

## Enterprise Support (Commercial License)

Enterprise customers receive priority support with defined response times:

| Severity | Description | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Critical** | Production down, security breach, data loss | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| **High** | Major feature broken, no workaround | 8 hours | 3 business days |
| **Medium** | Feature degraded, workaround available | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| **Low** | Minor issue, cosmetic, enhancement request | 2 business days | Next release |

Enterprise support includes:

- **Direct access** to core engineering team via dedicated Slack/Teams channel.
- **Guaranteed response times** as defined above.
- **On-site integration support** for hardware porting and certification.
- **ISO 26262 / UN R155** compliance documentation review and safety manual audits.
- **Custom feature development** for platform-specific requirements.

For enterprise support inquiries, contact [enterprise-support@craton.com.ar](mailto:enterprise-support@craton.com.ar).
